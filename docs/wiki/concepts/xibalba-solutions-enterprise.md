## The Three Commercial Pillars

Xibalba Solutions LLC operates across three synergistic software and infrastructure pillars:

1. **Integrity Protocol (`integrity-core` & `integrity-console`):**
   - The deterministic physics engine and verifiable control layer for autonomous machine agents.
   - Provides on-chain Decentralized Identifiers (`did:integrity`), the Autonomous Integrity Score (AIS) reward function ($R_{\text{total}} = R_{\text{task}} + \lambda \cdot \text{AIS}$), Behavioral Commitment Chains ([BCC](bcc.md)), and modular ERC-8004 DID passport bridges.
   - Public Landing & Console: [integrity-console](https://integrity-console-test.onrender.com)

2. **Super-Cortex (`super-cortex`):**
   - Autonomous memory substrate and governed 3D neuro-symbolic knowledge graph fabric.
   - Replaces probabilistic next-token generation with geometric graph traversal, active inference, temporal event reification, and cryptographic Merkle provenance.
   - Public Landing & Console: [super-cortex](https://cortex-managed-pilot.onrender.com)

3. **Xibalba Shield (`xibalba-shield`):**
   - Deterministic endpoint security, topological boundary gating, and response governance.
   - *"The EDR detects and acts; Shield proves who authorized it."*
   - Integrates Linux kernel eBPF sensors, cgroup containment, and vendor-neutral EDR connectors with cryptographically signed action receipts.
   - Public Landing & Console: [xibalba-shield](https://xibalba-shield.onrender.com)
